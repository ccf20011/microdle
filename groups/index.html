How to play

  
Sort the 16 tiles into 4 groups of 4 that share something: an organism trait, a drug class, a vector, a lab finding, even a name.

  
    
    
  • Tap 4 tiles, then Submit.
    • 
    
  • You can make 4 mistakes. "One away" means 3 of your 4 belong together.
    • 
    
  • Some tiles look like they fit more than one group. Only one answer uses every tile.
    •
